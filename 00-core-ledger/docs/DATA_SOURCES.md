# Data sources, assumptions and gaps

Checked 15 and 16 October 2026. **Status** is one of: SOURCED (a document was retrieved or quoted by a search result of that document), SECONDARY (confirmed only by press reports), ASSUMPTION (a policy parameter the team or a lender sets, not a fact), SIMULATED (generated by the prototype, labelled in the UI), GAP (not found; nothing is made up in its place).

## Deductions (config/rates.json)

All five rates below are **INDICATIVE placeholders** the team approved on 16-10-2026 so that statements and the seed can run. **Sourced figures replace them before the end-term.** Statements and the API show amounts computed from them, so any screenshot taken now carries this caveat.

| Deduction | Value used | Status | Basis and what is still missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold-storage rent | Rs 170 per quintal | SECONDARY | Midpoint of Rs 168 to 172 per quintal stated by an industry guide (indianpotato.com, "cold storage rental rates in india") as the West Bengal rate unchanged for four years as of February 2025; the same guide says an expert committee recommended Rs 190 (south Bengal) and Rs 194 (north Bengal) pending state approval. The state-notified rent for the season was not found; the Department of Agriculture or the West Bengal Cold Storage Association holds it. |
| Commission agent (arhatdar) | 7% of gross | ASSUMPTION | Midpoint of the 6 to 8 percent range in the team's mid-term report. The report is not in the repository; the figure was given by the team. Not a notified rate. |
| Market fee | 1.0% of gross | ASSUMPTION | Chosen by the team. The fee is set by notification under the West Bengal Agricultural Produce Marketing (Regulation) Act, 1972 (https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/14388/1/1972-35.pdf); the notified percentage was not found. |
| Hamali (loading and unloading) | Rs 8 per quintal (about Rs 4 per 50 kg bag) | ASSUMPTION | Chosen by the team. No published rate found. |
| Transport | Rs 30 per quintal | ASSUMPTION | Chosen by the team for Debra to Kolkata. A fifth value the team did not list but the statements need. In practice it comes from the vehicle quote at dispatch. |
| FPO margin | 2% of gross | ASSUMPTION | The FPO board's own decision; shown on every statement. |

Without these values `computeDeductions` refuses (ERR_VALIDATION, naming the missing rates); a test asserts that the shipped file claims no rate is SOURCED.

## Quality and weights (simulated devices)

| Item | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Paddy moisture, foreign matter, damaged grains | SOURCED | Government of India "Uniform Specification of all varieties of paddy": moisture 17.0%, foreign matter 1.0% inorganic and 1.0% organic, damaged, discoloured, sprouted and weevilled grains 5.0% (of which damaged, sprouted and weevilled at most 4%), immature and shrivelled 3.0%. Seen in the KMS 2016-17 and 2017-18 notifications (https://igmri.dfpd.gov.in/igmri/sites/default/files/KMS%202016-17.pdf) and the West Bengal paddy purchase specification of 2021 (https://epaddyarchive.wb.gov.in/ProcurementArchive2021/Notification/K2021%20paddy%20purchase%20unifrom%20specifications.pdf). The simulated paddy readings stay inside these limits: moisture 12.5 to 16.8%, foreign matter 0.2 to 1.0%, "brokenPct" 1.0 to 5.0% (loosely mapped to the 5% damaged-and-discoloured limit). Newer seasons may differ; the figures are those of the notifications retrieved. |
| Potato size, greening, rot and cut tolerances | **GAP** (SIMULATED) | No Indian grade standard (AGMARK or BIS) with mm size classes and tolerances could be retrieved; searches returned only US standards. An SFAC vegetable specification mentions an acceptable size of 40 to 80 mm but its grade tables are for onion. The simulated potato readings use plausible parameters (size class mix 55% 45-55 mm, 20% above 55, 20% 35-45, 5% below 35; greening 0 to 3%, rot 0 to 2%, cuts 0 to 3%) and are labelled simulated. |
| Bag weight 50 kg | **GAP** (verify) | Assumed for every commodity; to be verified against a West Bengal source before it is used in public copy. |
| Weighbridge net weight spread | SIMULATED | -2% to +1% of bags times 50 kg; tare 1,100 to 1,500 kg. Simulation parameters, not sourced. |
