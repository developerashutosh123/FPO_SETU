## Deductions (config/rates.json)

| Deduction | Status | What was found |
|---|---|---|
| Cold-storage rent per quintal | **GAP** | The West Bengal government's notified rent for the season was not found in any government document. An industry guide (indianpotato.com, "cold storage rental rates in india") says Rs 168 to 172 per quintal, unchanged for four years as of February 2025, and that an expert committee recommended Rs 190 (south Bengal) and Rs 194 (north Bengal) pending state approval. This is unverified and **not used**. The state Department of Agriculture or the West Bengal Cold Storage Association holds the authoritative figure. |
| Market fee | **GAP** | Levied under the West Bengal Agricultural Produce Marketing (Regulation) Act, 1972 (India Code: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/14388/1/1972-35.pdf). The percentage is set by notification against the Schedule and was not found. |
| Commission agent (arhatdar) commission | **GAP** | The Act defines the commission agent. No published rate found. |
| Hamali (loading and unloading) | **GAP** | No published rate found. |
| Transport | **GAP** | Depends on the vehicle quote; to be entered at dispatch. |
| FPO margin | ASSUMPTION, 2% | The FPO board's own decision; shown on every statement. |

**Consequence:** with these rates unsourced, `computeDeductions` refuses (ERR_VALIDATION, naming the missing rates), so no preview, no patronage run and no seed of settled lots can run on the real configuration. Tests and the throwaway-database e2e use `test/e2e-rates.json`, which says TEST on every line. The human decides: supply sourced values, or approve labelled indicative ones.

## Quality and weights (simulated devices)

| Item | Status | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Paddy moisture, foreign matter, damaged grains | SOURCED | Government of India "Uniform Specification of all varieties of paddy": moisture 17.0%, foreign matter 1.0% inorganic and 1.0% organic, damaged, discoloured, sprouted and weevilled grains 5.0% (of which damaged, sprouted and weevilled at most 4%), immature and shrivelled 3.0%. Seen in the KMS 2016-17 and 2017-18 notifications (https://igmri.dfpd.gov.in/igmri/sites/default/files/KMS%202016-17.pdf) and the West Bengal paddy purchase specification of 2021 (https://epaddyarchive.wb.gov.in/ProcurementArchive2021/Notification/K2021%20paddy%20purchase%20unifrom%20specifications.pdf). The simulated paddy readings stay inside these limits: moisture 12.5 to 16.8%, foreign matter 0.2 to 1.0%, "brokenPct" 1.0 to 5.0% (loosely mapped to the 5% damaged-and-discoloured limit). Newer seasons may differ; the figures are those of the notifications retrieved. |
| Potato size, greening, rot and cut tolerances | **GAP** (SIMULATED) | No Indian grade standard (AGMARK or BIS) with mm size classes and tolerances could be retrieved; searches returned only US standards. An SFAC vegetable specification mentions an acceptable size of 40 to 80 mm but its grade tables are for onion. The simulated potato readings use plausible parameters (size class mix 55% 45-55 mm, 20% above 55, 20% 35-45, 5% below 35; greening 0 to 3%, rot 0 to 2%, cuts 0 to 3%) and are labelled simulated. |
| Bag weight 50 kg | **GAP** (verify) | Assumed for every commodity; to be verified against a West Bengal source before it is used in public copy. |
| Weighbridge net weight spread | SIMULATED | -2% to +1% of bags times 50 kg; tare 1,100 to 1,500 kg. Simulation parameters, not sourced. |

## Other facts the contract asks for

| Whether potato is notified for e-NWR under WDRA | **GAP** | The WDRA FAQ (https://wdra.gov.in/faq) says 24 horticultural commodities are notified for cold-storage receipts; a 2012 circular named 26 and a later summary lists potato.
